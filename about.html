Our Story

About Actionspirit Camps

Actionspirit Camps was founded to help kids and teens experience adventure, build confidence, and create lifelong friendships. Our year-after-year community continues to grow because we focus on safety, belonging, and fun.

Mission & Values

We deliver a camp experience that helps every camper discover their strengths, take on new challenges, and feel part of a supportive community.

✔️ Positive mentorship and role modeling ✔️ Growth mindset and personal achievement ✔️ Safe, inclusive, and respectful culture

What Families Say

— Actionspirit Camp Parent

Experienced Leadership

Our camp directors and counselors are carefully selected for their experience, energy, and commitment to safety. Each counselor completes pre-season training on child development, activity instruction, and emergency response.

Certified Instructors

Specialists lead athletics, waterfront, and outdoor education activities.

Small Groups

Low camper-to-staff ratios ensure attention and meaningful connections.

Safety First

Daily check-ins, trained medical staff, and clear safety protocols.
